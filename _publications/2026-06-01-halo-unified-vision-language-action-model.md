- Contributing author on HALO (ICML 2026 Poster), focusing on π₀ baseline implementation, inference pipeline development, and real-robot evaluation.
-	Trained and evaluated the π₀ baseline model, implemented inference code, and compared baseline performance against HALO on embodied manipulation tasks.
-	Debugged and deployed robot experiments on the AgileX Mobile ALOHA bimanual platform, including environment setup, action execution, camera/robot synchronization, and failure-case analysis.
-	Evaluated real-world and OOD generalization experiments involving unseen objects, visual distractors, and lighting/textural variations.
